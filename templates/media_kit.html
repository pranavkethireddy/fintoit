One-liner
Fintoit is the AI CFO platform for startups.
Short (≈40 words)
Fintoit gives early-stage startups CFO-level financial intelligence (real-time burn rate, runway, cash-flow forecasting, and investor-ready reports), all powered by AI, without the cost of hiring a full-time CFO.
Long
Fintoit is a full-stack financial management platform built for early-stage startups. It brings real-time financial tracking, AI-powered insights, and CFO-grade tools (dashboards, cash-flow forecasting, scenario planning, one-click investor reports, accounting, payroll, and cap-table management) into a single web app, so founders can stay on top of their finances without hiring a CFO. Founded in 2026 and based in Plano, Texas.
